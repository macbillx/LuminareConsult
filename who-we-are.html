Luminare Consult offers research-informed consulting to groups and organizations working toward more equitable outcomes, effective leadership and inclusive work cultures.

We draw on sociology to understand how individual experiences connect to wider systems. That perspective helps organizations see patterns, ask better questions and choose practical next steps.

Based in Saskatchewan, Canada, Luminare works with organizations across sectors to support thoughtful, measurable progress.

Leadership

Guided by evidence.
Grounded in people.

Dr. Zita Seshie is the founding Executive Director of Luminare Consult. A sociologist, Equity, Diversity and Inclusion specialist, and community engagement advisor, Dr. Seshie brings research expertise in race, gender and the social determinants of health.

Under her guidance, Luminare brings an evidence-based approach to organizational and leadership development. The work helps groups and institutions move from intention to action with tangible, measurable outcomes.
