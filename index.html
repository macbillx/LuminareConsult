About Luminare

Better systems begin with understanding people.

Luminare Consult brings a sociologist’s lens to the complex challenges organizations face. We help groups move beyond good intentions with evidence-informed guidance rooted in how people, culture and systems intersect.

Led by Dr. Zita Seshie, our work turns insight into practical action—so teams can make better decisions, build trust and create lasting change.
